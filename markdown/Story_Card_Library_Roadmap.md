# Story Card Library Roadmap

This document defines the target size and organization of the initial
Story Card library.

These numbers are **targets**, not strict quotas. The goal is to build a
rich, well-balanced library of high-quality cards.

**Target Library Size:** \~250 Story Cards

  Theme             Target Cards
  --------------- --------------
  Relationships               25
  Identity                    20
  Community                   20
  Organizations               20
  History                     20
  Mystery                     20
  Politics                    20
  Economy                     20
  Conflict                    20
  Opportunity                 15
  Religion                    15
  Wilderness                  15
  Travel                      15
  Adventure                   15

**Total:** **250 Cards**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Theme Descriptions

### Relationships (25)

Connections between characters, NPCs, families, rivals, mentors, allies,
enemies, debts, promises, romance, and shared experiences.

### Identity (20)

Who a character is. Reputation, values, beliefs, failures, successes,
dreams, fears, habits, and defining moments.

### Community (20)

The people, customs, landmarks, and everyday life of settlements and
local regions.

### Organizations (20)

Guilds, noble houses, military orders, criminal syndicates, churches,
governments, schools, companies, and other influential groups.

### History (20)

Past events that shaped people, places, cultures, or the world.

### Mystery (20)

Unanswered questions, rumors, disappearances, legends, strange events,
hidden truths, and forgotten places.

### Politics (20)

Power struggles, leadership, diplomacy, succession, laws, corruption,
alliances, and governance.

### Economy (20)

Trade, businesses, professions, industries, resources, markets,
shortages, wealth, poverty, and commerce.

### Conflict (20)

Wars, feuds, rivalries, rebellions, disputes, disasters, and ongoing
tensions.

### Opportunity (15)

Adventure hooks, discoveries, jobs, rumors, expeditions, competitions,
and chances for advancement.

### Religion (15)

Faiths, temples, rituals, prophecies, sacred places, and religious
traditions.

### Wilderness (15)

Forests, mountains, rivers, ruins, monsters, natural wonders, and
unexplored places.

### Travel (15)

Roads, caravans, ships, inns, border crossings, journeys, and famous
routes.

### Adventure (15)

Treasures, ancient relics, villains, quests, dungeons, looming threats,
and campaign-defining events.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Generation Strategy

1.  Brainstorm 20--30 card concepts for the theme.
2.  Trim duplicates and weaker ideas.
3.  Generate JSON in batches of 5 cards.
4.  Review and refine.
5.  Finalize those cards.
6.  Continue until the theme is complete.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Guiding Philosophy

Story Cards are generic storytelling tools, not "Session 0 cards."

Story Decks determine **when** cards are used.

The cards themselves should never assume a particular point in the
campaign.
